How it works

You tell us what you depend on. We do the watching.

01

Point us at what you depend on

Give us a URL and the element or flow your code relies on — a button, a form, a nav path, whatever breaks your integration when it moves.

02

We watch it continuously

Structural changes, moved elements, renamed classes, shifted navigation. If it changes, we see it — you don't have to poll for it yourself.

03

You hear about it first

An alert lands in your inbox or your build, before your users notice anything broke and before the support tickets start.

Who it's for

Anyone whose software has a silent, unversioned dependency on someone else's website.

Extension developers

Your Chrome extension hooks into YouTube, Gmail, Notion, or another platform's DOM. Their redesign is your outage — and you usually find out from a one-star review.

Automation & agent builders

Your script or agent navigates a third-party dashboard to get work done. When that dashboard's layout shifts, your agent clicks the wrong thing, or nothing at all.

Anyone maintaining an unofficial integration

No public API, no changelog, no warning. Just a page you're quietly relying on to stay the same.
